Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SGnG handles information associated with its implemented website, account, communication, and Marketplace features.

Effective Date: November 8, 2026 Last Updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy is maintained separately from the SGnG Terms of Service so that privacy disclosures can remain aligned with the application's actual data practices.

1. Introduction

Strubell Goods & Gear (SGnG), a DBA of Strubell Publishing LLC, respects the privacy of users who interact with the SGnG website and Marketplace.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with SGnG's implemented website features and services.

2. Information Users Provide

Depending upon the application features used, users may provide information through:

  • Account registration and authentication.
  • Contact and customer-support forms.
  • Marketplace or order-related interactions.
  • Other application features that request user-provided information.

SGnG intends to maintain this policy so that it corresponds with data actually collected through implemented functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide requested website or Marketplace functionality.
  • Create, authenticate, and maintain eligible user accounts.
  • Receive and respond to user communications.
  • Support available Marketplace interactions.
  • Protect users, accounts, and application security.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve application operation.
  • Comply with applicable operational or legal requirements.

4. Accounts and Authentication

When account functionality is used, SGnG may process information necessary to create, authenticate, maintain, and secure eligible user accounts.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and should promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

5. Contact and Customer Communications

Information submitted through SGnG contact or customer-support functionality may be used to receive, process, and respond to the corresponding communication.

Communications may be retained when reasonably necessary to respond, document the interaction, maintain security, or satisfy applicable operational requirements.

6. Marketplace and Transaction Information

Where Marketplace transaction functionality is available, SGnG may process information reasonably necessary to support an applicable transaction.

Production payment-processing relationships and corresponding privacy practices will be documented when a provider is selected and implemented.

7. Cookies and Technical Information

SGnG may use technical functionality required to operate the application, including session-related technologies supporting security, authentication, and application behavior.

SGnG does not represent through this policy that optional advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies are in use unless those technologies are actually implemented.

8. Third-Party Services

SGnG may rely upon or link to services operated by third parties. Applicable providers may maintain separate terms, privacy practices, and security requirements.

Relevant providers will be identified as SGnG's production architecture and integrations are finalized.

9. Information Security

SGnG uses reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the application to help protect information under its control.

No Internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain secure credentials and devices and should report suspected account compromise.

10. Information Retention

SGnG intends to retain information for periods appropriate to the purpose for which it was processed and applicable operational or legal requirements.

More specific production retention practices will be documented as SGnG's operational procedures are finalized.

11. Privacy Choices and Rights

Users may contact SGnG concerning applicable privacy questions, account information, or privacy choices.

Additional rights or procedures may apply depending upon applicable law, the user's location, and the nature of the information involved.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SGnG may revise this Privacy Policy when its Marketplace functionality, data practices, technologies, providers, or applicable obligations change.

When material revisions are made, SGnG will update the Last Updated date and provide additional notice when appropriate or required.
