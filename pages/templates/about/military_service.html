The SGnG Story

🎖️ Military Service

Military service contributed principles of leadership, accountability, preparation, adaptability, and purposeful decision-making to the foundation of Strubell Goods & Gear.

Leadership and Accountability

Effective leadership requires clear communication, responsibility, thoughtful planning, and accountability for results. Those principles continue to influence how SGnG requirements and responsibilities are evaluated.

Preparation and Adaptability

Preparation provides a foundation, while adaptability supports effective decisions when requirements, constraints, or operating conditions change.

Problem Solving

SGnG applies a practical approach to problem solving by identifying the requirement, evaluating available capabilities, managing risk, and verifying the resulting solution.

Connection to SGnG

The lessons associated with service inform SGnG's emphasis on useful products, honest value, controlled scope, documentation, and continuous improvement.
